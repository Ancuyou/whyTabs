# Privacy Policy

Todo Kill stores all data locally in your browser using Chrome's local storage. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.

## Data We Store

- Task intent text you enter
- Timestamps and durations for each task
- Aggregate stats (killed count, minutes)
- Settings (bubble/indicator toggles, allowlist/blocklist)

## Data We Do NOT Collect

- No account information
- No browsing history uploads
- No analytics or telemetry

## Data Retention

- Tasks are automatically compacted: up to 1000 items
- Tasks older than 90 days are removed

## Export & Delete

- You can export data as JSON/CSV from the Options page
- You can delete data by clearing extension storage in Chrome

If you have questions, contact the developer listed on the Chrome Web Store page.
